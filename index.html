How to Play
Pick one fraction and place it in a territory. Legal moves earn Jack Points. Complete a territory to earn a big bonus. Go over, and your opponent hijacks the territory. Some fractions do not fit some models. If it does not fit, your turn ends. Highest Jack Points wins when all territories are claimed.
